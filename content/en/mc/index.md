## Playing on Wolfx Survival

::mc-feature-grid
:::mc-feature{title="No map resets"}
We keep the same map, so your builds and progress carry over.
:::
:::mc-feature{title="Vanilla mechanics"}
Redstone, farms, and exploration work much as they do in vanilla Minecraft.
:::
:::mc-feature{title="Game updates"}
We aim to follow the latest stable release when the server is ready.
:::
